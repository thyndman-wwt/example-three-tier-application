# A Verse on Code and Creation

## The Builder's Journey

In the silence of the screen,
Where logic flows and dreams convene,
A developer sits with fingers poised,
To craft a world from code and noise.

Layer upon layer, brick by brick,
The architecture grows, precise and quick,
Frontend dancing with vibrant hue,
Backend steady, strong and true.

Data flows like rivers wide,
Through databases where secrets hide,
Each query whispers ancient lore,
Of information kept in store.

The containers spin in Docker's grace,
Each service finding its rightful place,
Orchestrated in perfect time,
A symphony of code sublime.

From localhost to the world so vast,
The application built to last,
A testament to human thought,
The battles fought, the lessons taught.

In every function, every line,
A piece of passion, by design,
The three-tier tower stands so tall,
A beacon bright for one and all.

So here's to those who code and build,
With vision clear and hearts fulfilled,
May your applications soar and fly,
Reaching ever toward the sky.

---

*A celebration of the art and craft of software engineering.*
