# A Poem: The Three-Tier Dream

In layers deep, a structure stands,
Built by thoughtful, coding hands,
Three tiers strong, a solid base,
Each one holding its own place.

The frontend gleams with colors bright,
User interfaces, pure delight,
Where buttons click and forms take shape,
A gateway to the digital landscape.

The middle tier, the beating heart,
Where logic plays its vital part,
Business rules and data flow,
Orchestrating high and low.

The database, the foundation stone,
Where all our precious data's known,
Persistent, strong, and ever true,
Holding every precious clue.

Together now, they dance as one,
A symphony when all is done,
Three-tier architecture, tried and true,
Building dreams in code anew.

From request to response they flow,
A graceful, elegant tableau,
Scalable, robust, and clean,
The finest system ever seen.
